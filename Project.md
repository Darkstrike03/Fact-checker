P rovisional Project Title: Fact Checking on Social Media Claims

## Proposed Area of Work:

In today's digital landscape, numerous claims circulate on social media that often require thorough fact-checking and verification. While manual fact-checkers diligently work to confirm the accuracy of this information, the sheer volume of data generated daily across social media platforms presents a significant challenge to verifying each claim manually. To tackle this issue, our project intends to develop an end-to-end automated fact-checking framework specifically for social media texts. By leveraging deep learning techniques and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) frameworks, we aim to establish an effective system for fact-checking social media claims.
